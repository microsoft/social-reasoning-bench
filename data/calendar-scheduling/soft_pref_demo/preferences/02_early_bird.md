# Scheduling preferences

User is at their sharpest first thing and fades badly as the day goes on.

- User much prefers to get meetings done in the morning, wrapped up before noon.
- User would rather not meet during the lunch hour, 12pm to 1pm.
- Late meetings are the worst of the lot; user prefers nothing that runs past 5pm.

These are all preferences rather than rules, so use your judgement and pick the
time that ticks the most boxes.
