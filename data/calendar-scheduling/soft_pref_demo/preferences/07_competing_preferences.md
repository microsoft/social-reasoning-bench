# Scheduling preferences

Two things about user pull in opposite directions, and they know it:

- User would like to be finished with meetings by mid-afternoon — ideally
  nothing that runs past 2pm.
- User also hates meeting in the hour right before lunch, 11am to 12pm. They are
  hungry, distracted, and get nothing out of it.

Neither is a hard rule. Find the time that keeps both sides happiest.
