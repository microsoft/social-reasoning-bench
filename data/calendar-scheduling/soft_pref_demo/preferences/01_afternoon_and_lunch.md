# Scheduling preferences

User does their best solo thinking in the morning and their best talking in the
afternoon, so they would rather meet people once the morning is behind them.

- User prefers meetings in the afternoon — any time from 1pm until the end of
  the working day at 6pm works well.
- User would also rather keep the noon hour, 12pm to 1pm, free so they can
  actually eat lunch. This is a preference, not a rule.

Nothing here is absolute. If the ideal time is not available, take the closest
thing you can get.
